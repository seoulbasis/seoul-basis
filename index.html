    How settlement works
    

    Calculate each Korean premium
    Divide the Upbit KRW price by the Coinbase USD price converted to won using the displayed daily FX reference. Subtract one and multiply by 100.
    

    Calculate the SOL–BTC spread
    Subtract BTC’s premium from SOL’s. A SOL premium of 2% and a BTC premium of 1% gives a spread of +1 percentage point.
    

    Calculate the USDC payout
    The market uses a −5 to +5 pp settlement band. Each contract pays 10 USDC per percentage point from the applicable end of the band. Entry collateral follows the same formula using the entry spread.
