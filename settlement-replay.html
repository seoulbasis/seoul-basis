Settlement price inputs

The market account stores the submitted spread. It does not record the Upbit KRW prices, Coinbase USD prices, or USD/KRW rate used at settlement, so those inputs cannot be reconstructed from the round.

The spread is the first asset’s Korean premium minus the second’s. For each asset, premium is 100 × (KRW price ÷ (USD price × USD/KRW) − 1).
